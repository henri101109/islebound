ISLEBOUND

A fishing island you can live on, or race. Survival keeps your island. Speedrun throws that island away, starts a clock, and asks for one thing. The hub is only a place to stand with other people.

START

The title screen is the locker. Your body stands in the middle. Other people who are online stand in that same line. The rail on the right rotates through what is new, and through the black market.

  • Survival opens your island. Progress stays.
  • Speedrun opens the category tabs. The clock starts when you move. R resets the run.
  • Duels is a room where two players pick a list of categories and race them one after another.

H during play opens the short in-game walkthrough. This page is the long version. Esc from survival or a finished run returns to the title screen.

THE ISLAND

You wake by the dock. Dry ground is walked at full speed. Standing in the sea slows you and, if you stay under, starts to drown you. The wadable pond inland only wets your feet. It does not drown you.

Dock and shop

The harbor shop sells the skiff for 10 coins. If you cannot pay, the keeper says so. Stand near the keeper and press T while holding a gem or a crystal. The shop pays 5 for a gem and 25 for a crystal.

Beach

Crabs live on dry sand. Walk over one and press Space to scoop it. Most crabs go in the bag as food. A blue crab is a pet, and it is rare. In survival, each beach crab has a 1% chance to be blue. Scooping a blue one you do not already own grants the pet and equips it.

Shore wash

The tide leaves loot on the sand and takes it back. Shells, gems, crystals, and jellyfish wash in on a cycle. Walk into them to pick them up. If your bag is full, the spare stack can go to the satchel.

Inland

West of the dock, deep in the trees, is the temple. A river runs inland to a pool with a dinghy. Cliff mouths along that water are caves you can fish from on foot. Farther out, a wreck sits under the water.

BODY AND DEATH

You have 7 hearts. Dry walking speed is 165. In the sea it drops to 70. After you have been under for 1.6 seconds, you lose a heart every 0.85 seconds. At 0 hearts you are dead for 1.4 seconds, then you wake at the spawn with full hearts, off any boat. A boat, the pond, and the sky draught keep you from drowning.

Survival loot no longer raises a level. The number people see on you is your career rank, and that only moves when you finish speedruns.

BAG AND SATCHEL

The bag is eight slots, chosen with 1 through 8. Stacks have a maximum. Cosmetics that land in the bag are learned by the locker. They are equipped from the title screen, not from the island.

The satchel is a second eight slots. Press E in survival to open it. Drag between the bag and the satchel. The E hint sits to the left of the bag even when your hands are empty.

Indoors, Enter drops whatever you are holding.

COINS

Coins are the island’s money. You earn them by selling to a keeper, by opening a treasure chest with the cabin master, and by a few special finds. The dock shop buys stones. The fishing cabin buys fish. Rome’s street sells food, and its alleys sell draughts, the sea rod, and the Egypt cat.

BuyerPays
Dock shopGem 5, crystal 25
Fishing cabinThe catch list below. Hold the fish and press T.

A treasure chest in the bag does nothing until you find a treasure master. Bring it to the cabin and press T. A classic opening pays somewhere between 100 and 500 coins.

BOATS

Skiff

Buy it at the dock for 10 coins, then walk onto it. Enter boards and leaves. Leaving puts you back on the T-head of the dock. The boat must be sitting still before you can cast. You can cast from the skiff with empty hands. A sea rod mounted on the boat is only a perk: bites come sooner (the wait is multiplied by 0.82) and the rare and legendary bands are a little fatter.

Dinghy

The river pool inland has a dinghy. It is the boat for the pond. Eel, leech, and a lot of blowfish live there. Enter on the dinghy hops you back to the dock’s T-head.

FISHING

Press C or F to cast. You cannot cast while the serpent is up, or while a pickup animation is still playing.

Where you can cast

  • Skiff. Barehanded is allowed. The boat has to be still. Ocean casts wait about 13.5 seconds before the bite window, longer in bad weather.
  • Dinghy. The pond. Shorter wait, about 3 to 7 seconds.
  • Shore. Stand on the sand with your feet at the water. In survival you must be holding a rod. Speedrun fish categories let you cast barehanded.
  • Pond wade. Stand in the inland pool.
  • Cave. Stand in a cliff mouth on the pond. Glow fish and the angler live here.

Ocean odds and distance

From the skiff, how far you are from the dock changes the tier, not which fish inside a tier. Every fish in a tier is equally likely.

Between 4 and 12 tiles the odds slide from the near table to the far table. Commons are cod, trout, salmon, and jellyfish. Rares are crab, mini shark, and green jellyfish. Legendaries are shark, white jellyfish, and treasure chest.

Bait in your hand

Holding cod, trout, salmon, jellyfish, or a crab while you cast is good bait. It raises the legendary band by a quarter and the rare band by half, and it makes super-legendaries 10% more likely. The bait is used up when the catch resolves.

Holding a Heart of the Sea on an ocean cast guarantees a blowfish. The heart is not used up. Holding a shark is a 50% chance of a megalodon. A mini shark is 25%. The shark is only eaten if the megalodon actually bites.

Super-legendaries

After the normal roll, each of these is rolled on its own. If more than one hits, one of the hits is picked at random.

  • Gold jellyfish, 1 in 200
  • Blowfish, 1 in 250
  • Rainbow jellyfish, 1 in 300
  • Megalodon, 1 in 500

Other waters

The pond, before a storm override: 10% portal leech, 12% waterfall eel, 25% blowfish, otherwise an even split of cod, trout, and salmon.

The shore: 12% hammerhead, then stingray until 40%, otherwise a mix of cod, trout, salmon, stingray, and hammerhead. Good bait tightens that roll.

A cave: 10% angler, then lantern fish until 42%, otherwise glow minnows and lantern fish.

A mounted sea rod on the boat multiplies legendary odds by 1.2, rare odds by 1.25, and bite chance by 1.08.

WEATHER

Storms exist in survival, and in Storm%. Everywhere else the sky stays clear. Clear weather lasts a long time, somewhere between 6 and 15 minutes. When it breaks, it is usually another clear stretch. Sometimes a short squall. A real storm is uncommon, and a cyclone after a storm is rare.

Squall, storm, and cyclone each last only a few seconds to half a minute. While a cyclone is up, every cast has a 14% chance to be a storm fish before the normal table runs. Storms also stretch the wait before a bite: a little in a squall, more in a storm, most in a cyclone. Storm% locks the sky on a cyclone for the whole run.

THE SERPENT

Hold a sea shell and press Space while you are on your own skiff, more than eight tiles from the dock, and not in the dead of night. The serpent has 5 hearts and a body of 9 segments. The day clock pauses while it is here. You cannot cast. Steer the boat to dodge. When it breaches, strike with the wand. A cloak on your back confuses it.

There is a 45 second cooldown after a summon. Beating it drops a Heart of the Sea. That heart is the end of Serpent%, and it is also the bait that forces a blowfish on later ocean casts.

TEMPLE

The temple is west of the dock, inland, in the trees. Inside is a large maze with no traps. The jade mask sits on a pedestal at the far end. Touching that pedestal is the whole of Mask%. You do not need an empty bag slot for the run to count.

In survival the mask is a face cosmetic. Equipping it throws jade and gold sparks around you. It is also worth a small burst of the old item XP the first time it is picked up.

THE WRECK

Under the water there is a dive. Sharks in the lower rooms will shove you back toward the sub if you touch them. In the wreck itself an octopus pushes you if you stand too close and you are not walking away. The sunken chest at the end can be taken once. It is a single item, separate from the beach treasure chest.

ROME

A portal in the river ruins opens onto a city. The avenue runs north to a palace. People in the street talk about the king, the races, olives, and the gate. The portal home sits at the south end of the avenue.

Street stalls

Olives are 4 coins, bread is 3, wine is 8. They stack in the bag and are mostly food and color.

Alley dealers

The alleys are not marked on a sign. The dealers stand in the gaps between the blocks.

  • Tar draught, 6 coins
  • Mend draught, 8 coins
  • Sky draught, 14 coins
  • Mystery draught, 10 coins
  • Sea rod, 45 coins
  • Egypt cat, 950 coins

By the palace steps, and only while a mystery draught is still in you, a dealer sells the Jorts skin for 1942 coins. He does not advertise. If you already own a skin or a pet, he will not sell it again.

The 1500 meters

The king’s racing line is exactly 1500 meters. At a dry walk of 165, a perfect line is 3 minutes and 29.89 seconds. An animal pocket sits on the course. The clock pauses while that gate is shut and starts again when it opens, so waiting at a closed gate does not cost you the pause. Winning the race grants the Crown of the Kingdom. It is not sold anywhere.

Kings Race% starts you on that line. Full Kings% starts you on a fresh island. You have to reach the portal yourself, then walk the city. Any finish of the line counts for the race category.

POTIONS

Press Space while a draught is selected. The four Rome draughts last 60 seconds.

  • Red potion. Heals 3 hearts at once. It is not one of the timed draughts.
  • Tar draught. Your legs drag. Movement smears behind you until it lets go.
  • Mend draught. One heart immediately, then another heart every 6 seconds while it lasts, up to 7.
  • Sky draught. You leave the ground, on the island or in Rome. Water stops drowning you. When it ends, the game pulls you back onto ground you can stand on.
  • Mystery draught. The world stops being reliable. Drinking it ends Trip%. It is also the only way the Jorts dealer will talk to you.

PETS

Pets are equipped in the locker, on the Pets tab. In survival the equipped pet follows you. It never dies. If you get more than 260 pixels away it warps back to you. P tells it to sit. A speedrun pet, from Cat% or Crab%, exists only for that run. It is not written into your locker.

  • Blue crab. No vendor. In survival, 1% of beach crabs are blue. Scoop one you do not own.
  • Egypt cat. 950 coins from the alley dealer in Rome. Faster than the crab when it follows.

SKINS AND LOOKS

The locker is the title screen. Skins, gear, and pets are separate tabs. Drag a face or a back piece onto the sockets, or click a skin to wear it. Jakob always wears the Norway flag on his back. Bolt always wears the Jamaica flag. Those backs cannot be taken off while that skin is on.

Skins you start with

Classic, Jakob, Joe, and the alien. Anyone who already had a save when skins arrived received this set once.

Skins you earn

Bolt, Crew, Homer, Indy, Flash, and Lex. Each one is a quest in Rewards. See that section for the exact time.

A skin you buy

Jorts, 1942 coins, palace steps, mystery draught only.

Face and back

  • Jade mask, from the temple
  • Straw hat, crown, and beta crown, from the weekly reel
  • Cloak, worn on the back. Space turns it on and off. This is what Invis Shark% asks for.
  • Ember cape, from the world
  • Job App cloak, for finishing Invis Shark%
  • American cloak, for making an account
  • Jellyfish cape, for a fast White Jelly%
  • Crown of the Kingdom, for winning the race

CAREER RANK

Career rank is the level under your name. It moves only when a speedrun finishes. A faster run, a new personal best, a submitted time, and a world record all pay more. The category itself has a weight, so a leech run is worth more than a death run even at the same pace. The curve is steep on purpose. Level 1 to 20 is bronze, 21 to 35 is silver, 36 to 55 is gold, 56 to 75 is diamond, and 76 and up is champion. The number can keep climbing past 76. The display stops at 200.

Roughly, a finish pays a small base times the category weight times how you compared to the current world record. Submitting adds about three quarters again. A personal best adds another chunk. A world record adds a larger one. Survival fishing does not add career XP.

WEEKLY REWARDS

An account is required. Each week you get three free spins. The result is chosen before the reel moves. Half the time it is the straw hat, 30% the crown, 20% the beta crown. When the three are gone, the button says next week.

ACCOUNTS, RANKS, AND PEOPLE

Names

A board name is 3 to 12 characters, letters and numbers. Accounts wear a gold name. Guests wear the plain one. The same person is folded together when the names are aliases of each other, so a short name and a longer one can share a row.

Why an account

An account keeps your locker and your island on the server, so a cleared browser does not wipe you. It also grants the VIP tag, unlocks the weekly reel, and is how media tags get attached to you. Guests can still play, finish runs, and type a name on the result screen.

Tags

  • Owner. Henri only.
  • VIP. Any account.
  • MVP. A support purchase, marked at $4.99.
  • YouTuber. A long video at 1,000, or a short at 30,000.
  • TikTok. Over 20,000 views.
  • Twitch. A stream with 20 or more viewers.

Hub

The hub is a shared plaza. You see whoever is online, with their name, their look, and their tag. E sends an emote. Tab is chat. The door or Esc leaves. Chat messages are short, and you cannot send them faster than about one every second and a half.

Profiles

Profiles on the title screen searches people who have played on this boards setup. A green dot means they were seen recently.

SPEEDRUN

Pick a category. The world is a fresh island. The clock starts when you move. R resets. When the goal happens, the time locks. You can type a name and post it. A replay may be stored with that run. Esc from the result can return to the categories or the title screen.

The list is five tabs. Nothing scrolls. A and D switch tabs. Up and down move the highlight. The board on the right is the top 10 for whatever is highlighted. VID plays that row’s replay.

Catch

The run ends when that thing is in hand, or, for the chest, when it opens.

  • Shark%. Catch a shark.
  • White Jelly%. Catch a white jellyfish.
  • Chest%. Open the treasure chest.
  • Invis Shark%. Catch a shark while the cloak is on and the rod is idle.
  • Blowfish%. Catch a blowfish. The clock includes the heart effect and one extra second.
  • Megalodon%. Catch a megalodon.
  • Eel%. Catch a waterfall eel. The dinghy on the inland pool is the usual boat.
  • Leech%. Catch a portal leech, from that same pond.
  • Crab%. Scoop a blue crab off the beach. In this category the beach is much more likely to have one, and if none rolled, one is forced.
  • Angler%. Catch an angler from a cave mouth.
  • Storm%. Catch a storm fish. The sky is locked on a cyclone.
  • Hammer%. Catch a hammerhead from the shore.

Stat

  • 10 Coins%, 25 Coins%, 100 Coins%. Reach that many coins.
  • Death%. Drown. The run ends when you wake up from the death, not when the first heart drops.
  • Full Inv%. Fill all eight bag slots with real items. A cosmetic in a slot does not count.
  • Boat%. Buy the skiff and get on it.

Adventure

  • Mask%. Reach the jade pedestal at the end of the temple maze.
  • Serpent%. Take the Heart of the Sea off the serpent.

Race

  • Kings Race%. You start on the 1500 meter line. Finish it.
  • Full Kings%. Start on the island, reach Rome yourself, and walk the city through the race.

Dimension

  • Cat%. Buy the Egypt cat from the Rome alley. The run’s cat is temporary.
  • Trip%. Drink a mystery draught.

BOARDS AND REPLAYS

Each category has a top 10, fastest first. Your result screen shows the time, whether it was a personal best, and a place to type a name if you are not already locked to an account name. Posting sends the time. A replay of the run can be saved with it.

On the category screen, a row with a replay shows VID. Clicking it spectates that exact run. Esc cuts the replay and puts you back on the board.

Stats, on the title screen, has two sides. Survival tracks playtime, level, sharks, chests, and your highest coin count. Speedrun tracks playtime, runs, and your personal bests.

SAVING

Survival writes the island on its own, about every second and a half, and again when you leave. The locker (skins, pets, quests, career) is saved with it. If you are signed in, that copy is also sent to your account, so another browser can pick it up.

A newer save does not always win. A fresh island, a speedrun, or a replay is not allowed to replace an island that is further along. Level, experience, the jade mask, the sunken chest, and the boat are the things that count as progress. If the live island would go backwards, the further one is kept and loaded instead.

Speedrun pets and speedrun loadouts are not written into the locker.
